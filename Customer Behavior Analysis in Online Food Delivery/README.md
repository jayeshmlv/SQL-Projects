# Customer Behavior Analysis in Online Food Delivery | SQL Project

## Overview

This SQL project analyzes customer purchasing behavior in an online food delivery platform. The objective is to understand customer spending patterns, product preferences, and the impact of Gold Membership on customer engagement and revenue.

---

## Business Objective

- Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
- Measure the impact of Gold Membership on sales.
- Identify high-value customers and popular products.
- Generate business insights to improve customer retention and revenue.

---

## Tools & Skills

- MySQL
- SQL
- Joins
- CTEs
- Window Functions
- Aggregate Functions
- Data Analysis

---

## Key Insights

- Gold members spend significantly more than regular customers.
- Customer engagement increases after joining the Gold Membership program.
- A small number of products contribute to the majority of revenue.
- Customers exhibit distinct purchasing patterns that can be used for personalization.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Promote Gold Membership to increase customer lifetime value.
- Identify and reward high-value customers with exclusive offers.
- Recommend products based on customer purchase history.
- Focus marketing efforts on top-performing products to maximize revenue.
